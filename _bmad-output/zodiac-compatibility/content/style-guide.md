## 1. Voice and tone

- **Friendly, warm, clear.** Expert but unpretentious: explain the pair the way you would to a bright friend over tea. Short-to-medium sentences (aim ≤ 30 words), concrete images, no jargon walls.
- **Address the couple as "the two of you"** when a second-person moment is needed. Most of the time, describe the pair in the third person by sign name: "Aries sprints while Taurus strolls."
- **Growth framing at every band.** Difficulty is "challenging", "demanding", "growth-intensive", "needs patience" — never "doomed", "toxic", "dangerous", "hopeless". Every low-band block ends with a concrete, doable path forward.
- **Specific over generic.** A sentence that could describe any couple is a defect (see the swap test, §5).
- **British English spelling** (neighbour, favourite, honour, candour, savoured) — matches Vyotish's internal documents.
- Capitalize sign names and planet names (Aries, Taurus, Mars, Venus, the Sun); write elements, modalities and aspects lowercase (fire, cardinal, the square angle).

## 2. Person, neutrality, symmetry

**R1 — Gender and orientation neutrality (hard).** Cards must read identically for any couple. Banned for partners: he, she, him, her, man, woman, husband, wife, boyfriend, girlfriend, bride, groom. Use instead: "the two of you", "both", "each", "one partner … the other …", and singular-they for a sign personified ("Leo hears criticism of anything they have built personally").

**R2 — Symmetry (hard).** Stored copy is rendered for both orderings (`/compatibility/aries/taurus` and `/compatibility/taurus/aries`) from the same record. Therefore:

- Never write "your partner", "your sign", "you are the Aries here", or any mapping of reader → sign.
- Anchor traits to the signs by name: "Aries sprints, Taurus strolls" — valid in either direction.
- "One partner … the other …" is allowed only once the traits are already anchored to the signs elsewhere in the same block.
- Same-sign records are trivially symmetric; write "two Aries", "both Aries partners".

**R3 — No relationship-stage or age assumptions (hard).** No marriage pressure, no weddings, no children, no "too young/too old", no stage assumptions (new vs long-term). Friendship copy must stand alone for platonic readers.

## 3. Length spec (exact)

| Block | Spec |
|---|---|
| `overall.verdict` | 1–2 sentences, ~25–45 words. The pair's signature dynamic in one breath: the structure (aspect/element/modality), the promise, the work. |
| each of the 6 categories | 2–4 sentences, ~35–70 words |
| `tips` | 4–6 items; each ≤ 20 words |

No scores, percentages, star ratings, rankings or invented numbers anywhere in the copy — the engine computes and displays scores; writers never state them. Degrees (30°/90°/180°) are permitted sparingly (at most once per record), but plain-language aspect names are preferred ("the square angle", "opposite ends of the wheel").

## 4. Category recipe — what each block is for

| Block | Covers | Keeps out |
|---|---|---|
| `love` | Romantic attraction and affection: how the pair falls for each other, how devotion is expressed, the romantic mood of the bond. | Not the physical spark (`sexual`), not inner feelings (`emotional`). |
| `sexual` | Chemistry and passion: attraction, tempo and energy between them. Tasteful, attraction/chemistry/passion framing, explicit-free — no body parts, no act descriptions. | Not romance (`love`), not feelings (`emotional`). |
| `friendship` | The platonic read: loyalty, teamwork, shared time — how the bond holds outside romance. Must carry standalone value for a non-romantic reader ("can we just be friends?"). | Not a romance recap. |
| `communication` | How words travel: speaking and listening styles, debate patterns, decision-talk, misunderstanding loops. | Not reliability (`trust`), not feelings (`emotional`). |
| `trust` | Reliability and faith: promises kept, consistency, honesty over time, whether each can depend on the other. | Must NOT re-explain communication — trust is about believing and depending, not about talking. |
| `emotional` | Inner weather: how each sign processes feelings, moods, intuition, emotional safety and attunement. | Must NOT re-explain love — this is the interior angle, not the romance angle. |

**Disambiguation rules** — if a sentence fits two blocks equally, reassign by lane or rewrite:

- Love vs Emotional: the relationship-angle → Love; the inner-angle → Emotional.
- Love vs Sexual: affection and devotion → Love; spark, desire and pace → Sexual.
- Trust vs Communication: how words travel → Communication; whether each can rely on the other → Trust.
- Friendship vs Love: write Friendship so it still works if the romance were absent.

**Category tendencies (from pair-matrix §7) — copy must agree with the displayed category scores:**

| Tendency | Copy implication |
|---|---|
| Love = overall +4 (every pair) | Attraction is the method's strongest suit; Love runs slightly warmer than the overall story, even in Challenging pairs. |
| Sexual = +6 for conjunction/trine/sextile/opposition, else +2 | Chemistry usually outperforms the overall. Even the hardest squares get live Sexual copy — gated on safety and pacing, not dead. |
| Friendship = +2 same/complementary element, −2 contrary element | Fire–water and earth–air pairs get the most strained Friendship copy. |
| Communication = −6 for square/quincunx/semi-sextile | The friction zone for those three aspects — carry the friction honestly here. |
| Trust = −8 for opposition/square, else −2 | The friction zone for oppositions and squares. |
| Emotional = +2 water-involving | Water pairs earn a depth/attunement note. |

Writers: make the weak-spot categories carry the friction. Friction-free Communication copy on a square contradicts the page's own numbers.

## 5. Grounding rule — every sentence stands on the pair's facts

**Workflow (per pair):**

1. Look up the row in pair-matrix §4 (canonical, wheel-order pair). Extract: aspect, element pair, modality pair, polarity, rulers, ruler-affinity note, band.
2. If the pair appears in §6, read the ruling — that nuance belongs in the copy (e.g., "shared Mercury", "mutual detriment", "dignity pull-back, band retained").
3. Note the §7 tendencies (table above) so weak-spot categories carry friction.
4. Write to the length spec, weaving **at least two structural facts** into the record — ideally one inside the overall verdict.

**Weaving vocabulary** (pick what the row supports): "two cardinal signs", "two fixed signs", "the water trine", "shared Mercury", "opposite signs on the relationship axis", "cardinal fire meets fixed fire", "Mars-ruled drive", "Venus-ruled grace", "next-door neighbours on the wheel", "the square angle", "the self/other axis".

**Dignity notes are tradition, not fact about people.** Hedge them: "classically, Leo's Sun is said to be exalted in Aries", "an old image of …", "a classical reading is …". No astrology-as-science claims; no "studies show".

**The swap test (mandatory).** Mentally replace your two sign names with any other pair of the same band. If the copy still works unchanged, it is generic filler — rewrite it around the pair's actual structure. Every record should contain at least one image or dynamic that could belong to no other pair ("two crowns competing for one stage", "the sprint and the stroll", "stored hurt grows in silence").

## 6. Band-to-tone map

| Band | Register |
|---|---|
| Excellent (85–95) | Celebrate openly, then name the one honest "trap of ease" (taking each other for granted, competing crowns). No "perfect". |
| Strong (70–84) | Warm celebration plus one growth note. |
| Mixed (55–69) | Lead with the genuine pull (most Mixed pairs are magnetic oppositions), name the recurring friction, hand the reader the lever (turn-taking, explicit agreements, deadlines). |
| Challenging (40–54) | "Different tempos / different instincts" framing; effortful but not hostile; a concrete bridge in every block. |
| Very challenging (35–39) | Gentlest register. Name the friction plainly, frame it as growth-intensive, give the most concrete practices. "With patience / with practice / slowly" do a lot of work here. Zero fear language. |

Superlatives: only the "one of the …" form, used sparingly, and only where the band clearly supports the register ("one of the more demanding pairings" is fair for a ≤40 score; "one of the warmest matches" for a top trine). "The best/worst match in the zodiac" is banned.

## 7. Banned words and claims (hard sweep)

**Banned tokens (AC-Q2 sweep):** always, never, guaranteed, destined, destiny, doomed, toxic, "perfect match", proven, scientifically, "will happen". Watch tips especially — imperative advice gravitates toward these words; write "make it a habit", "keep", "try", "let", "trade" instead.

**Also banned (soft list, treat as hard):** soulmate, twin flame, "made for each other", "the best/worst match", absolute "will + outcome" statements.

**Claim classes banned outright:** medical, mental-health, pregnancy/fertility, legal and financial claims. Money or work may appear only inside a generic tip, with no astrological causation ("agree on a pace for spending" is fine; "Taurus brings wealth" is not). Astrology is presented as an interpretive tradition, never as science.

**Hedging vocabulary:** may, often, tends to, can, sometimes, usually, "a common reading", "classically", "is said to", "in classical imagery".

## 8. Disclaimer framing

The platform injects the "for guidance and entertainment purposes only" disclaimer near the score and in the footer — **do not write it into records**. Copy may allude to reflection and self-awareness framing ("worth reflecting on", "a lens for both of you", "use it as a prompt for a conversation"). Verification flags and method notes are also platform-side; the copy need not restate them.

## 9. Tips rules

- 4–6 tips per record; each ≤ 20 words.
- Form: imperative ("Trade the crown weekly") or gentle suggestion ("Try …", "It helps to …").
- Every tip must be specific to the pair's dynamics. "Communicate openly" is a fail. Anchor at least one tip to the pair's signature structure (two cardinal signs and the wheel, the tempo gap, the trust-building lever).
- Tips are the natural home for practical, kind advice — include one habit-style tip, and where the band is Challenging/Very challenging, one repair-style tip (what to do after friction).
- The banned-word sweep applies to tips.

## 10. Sign-binding rule (automated QA)

Every rendered pair page must mention exactly its two signs by name (story AC-Q2; the templating-bug lesson, research §5.8). Therefore, per record:

1. **Requirement:** both sign names appear at least once across the record. Do not rely on pronouns or "one partner / the other" alone.
2. **Recommended:** name both signs inside the `overall.verdict` itself, and give each sign at least two mentions across the record.
3. Same-sign records: the single name appears multiple times ("two Aries", "both Aries partners") — trivially binding.

## 11. Format and file layout

- One JSON file per lower-index sign: `content/pairs/{sign}.json`. The file holds that sign's pairs against every partner of equal or higher wheel index, records ordered by ascending partner index:
  - `aries.json` = aries-aries, aries-taurus, …, aries-pisces (12 records)
  - `taurus.json` = taurus-taurus, …, taurus-pisces (11 records) … `pisces.json` = pisces-pisces (1 record). 78 records total.
- The file root is a **JSON array** even while partial. Writers completing a partial file **insert** new records in partner-index order — do not blindly append.
- Record schema (exact; no extra keys; no scores):

```json
{
  "pair": "aries-leo",
  "aspect": "trine",
  "overall": { "verdict": "1-2 sentence original summary of the pair's dynamic" },
  "categories": {
    "love": "2-4 sentences",
    "sexual": "2-4 sentences",
    "friendship": "2-4 sentences",
    "communication": "2-4 sentences",
    "trust": "2-4 sentences",
    "emotional": "2-4 sentences"
  },
  "tips": ["tip", "tip", "tip", "tip"]
}
```

- `pair`: lowercase `sign1-sign2`, wheel-order sorted (lower index first).
- `aspect` ∈ `conjunction | semisextile | sextile | square | trine | quincunx | opposition` (JSON token). In prose you may write "semi-sextile" or a plain description ("the next-door-neighbour angle").
- Strings: plain prose, UTF-8, no HTML/markdown/emoji, no line breaks inside strings. Apostrophes are fine; avoid double quotes inside strings (use single quotes when quoting a suggested phrase). No trailing commas.

## 12. Before / after rewrites (voice calibration)

**A — fear → growth register:**

- Before (reject): "Aries and Cancer are a terrible match — this relationship is doomed to constant fighting."
- After (accept): "Aries and Cancer square off as bold fire and protective water, and the friction is real: Aries speaks to solve, Cancer speaks to feel heard. With patience, that same friction can teach boldness some gentleness and sensitivity some directness."

**B — generic → grounded:**

- Before (reject): "These two signs communicate well and can work through anything together."
- After (accept): "Aries argues to reach a decision, while Libra argues to hold every perspective in view — two cardinal styles circling one topic. Agreeing that decisions get a deadline turns that loop into teamwork."

**C — directional → symmetric:**

- Before (reject): "Your Taurus partner will need a lot of patience with your impulsive side."
- After (accept): "Aries sprints and Taurus strolls; the pairing works when the sprinter enjoys the stroll and the stroller respects the sprint."

## 13. Writer self-QA checklist (run before submitting each batch)

1. Both sign names appear (verdict-level naming recommended; each sign ≥ 2 mentions).
2. Banned-word sweep clean — including tips (always, never, guaranteed, destined, destiny, doomed, toxic, perfect, proven, scientifically, will + outcome, soulmate).
3. No gendered or directional phrasing (he/she/his/her, man/woman, husband/wife, "your partner", "you are the …").
4. No scores, percentages, stars, rankings or invented numbers.
5. Length spec met: verdict 25–45 words; categories 2–4 sentences / 35–70 words; tips 4–6 × ≤ 20 words.
6. At least 2 structural facts woven; swap test passes; at least one pair-unique image.
7. Category lanes respected; weak-spot categories carry the friction per the §7 tendency table.
8. Sexual copy tasteful and distinct from Love; Emotional is inner weather, not romance; Friendship stands alone; Trust is not Communication redux.
9. Astro claims hedged ("is said to", "classically"); no science, medical, legal or financial claims; no stage or age assumptions.
10. Register matches the band-tone map; low-band blocks end with a doable path forward.
11. Valid JSON, exact schema, correct aspect token, correct file and slot ordering.
