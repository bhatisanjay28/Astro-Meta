Aries Horoscope — Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Aries horoscope based on the Sun sign or the Moon sign?

This horoscope is based on your Moon sign (chandra rashi), not your Sun sign — in Vedic astrology the daily rashifal is read from the Moon's sign. Use the Birth Rashi calculator to find your Moon sign.

How often is the Aries (Mesha) daily horoscope updated?

The Aries horoscope is updated every day — each day's forecast is computed from that day's planetary transits (Chandra gochar) relative to your Moon sign.

What does today's Aries horoscope cover?

It covers nine areas — career, business, finance, love, marriage, family, children, health and education — plus do's & don'ts, lucky colour, number & direction, auspicious timings, a remedy, and the deity of the day.
