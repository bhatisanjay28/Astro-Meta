Saturn governs business this year from its most demanding angle — heavier compliance, slower payments, thinner margins for error. The strategy is protective: cut fixed costs before cutting quality, keep no single client indispensable, document every agreement before starting. Avoid new debt and major launches; this is a structure-and-survival year. Where obligations feel heavy, renegotiate early and honestly rather than defaulting quietly. Keep the smallest reliable revenue lines alive — they are bridges. Work with an advisor on taxes and compliance so nothing compounds.
