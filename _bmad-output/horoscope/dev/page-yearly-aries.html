How we compute this

How we compute this: We take each planet's sidereal position (Lahiri ayanamsha) at 00:05 IST, count the house it occupies from your Vedic Moon sign, and apply classical gochara (transit) favourability tables — including vedha (obstruction) adjustments. Lucky colour, number, direction, mantra and deity follow the day's ruling planet by a fixed traditional mapping. Planetary nodes are computed by the standard mean-node method. Ratings are band labels, not scores; they describe timing tendencies, not outcomes.
