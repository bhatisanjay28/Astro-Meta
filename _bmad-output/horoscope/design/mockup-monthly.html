Overview — December 2026

▼ Challenging · चुनौतीपूर्ण

December is a month of two tempos. Until the Sun enters Sagittarius on Dec 16, keep commitments lean and routines warm; after the shift, energy becomes easier to direct, and the last week suits planning for the new year. Education is December's clear bright spot — the month favours learners at every level.

Mercury crosses into Scorpio on Dec 3 and into Sagittarius on Dec 22, so written work goes deepest early and widest late in the month. The new moon on Dec 9 is a natural pause for rest; the full moon on Dec 24 brings matters to a visible head just before the holidays.
