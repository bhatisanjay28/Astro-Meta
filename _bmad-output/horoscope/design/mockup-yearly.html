Overview — 2027

▼ Challenging · चुनौतीपूर्ण

2027 is a building year. Saturn spends it close to your sign — first in your 12th, then in your 1st from June — which in tradition is a phase that rewards steady effort, simple routines and patience more than speed. The good news runs alongside: Jupiter moves through your 5th house twice (until January and again June–November), lifting study, creative work and family joy in those stretches; Rahu stays in your 10th house all year, quietly rewarding consistent, visible effort at work. Love is the steadiest area of the year; education picks up strongly from July. The year asks you to build slowly and keep going — the Aries way of doing exactly that, with a longer breath.

Sade Sati note

Saturn moves through the rising phase of Sade Sati (from your 12th house) during Jan–May and Nov–Dec, and the peak phase (your 1st house) Jun–Oct. Tradition reads this as a season that rewards steady effort and simple routines — slow building, not sudden change.
